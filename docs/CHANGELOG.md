Changelog
=========

1.0.0 (November 4, 2026)
------------------------
- Initial version: cleaners for JPEG, PNG, WebP, GIF, MP4/MOV/3GP; upload hooks; log table with outcome of each run.
- Audio (M4A, MP3, AAC, FLAC, Ogg, Opus) and WebM/MKV videos; videos inside JPEG and HEIC motion photos are removed.
- DNG and other TIFF-based camera raw images (CR2, NEF, ARW, ORF, RW2...); WAV, AIFF, AVI, WMV, PSD/PSB, JPEG 2000 (.jp2) and JPEG XL (.jxl) files.
- PDF, Office (DOCX, XLSX, PPTX) and OpenDocument document properties; SVG metadata and scripts.
- Translations: English, Italian, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese and Simplified Chinese.
- Requires HumHub 1.19.
